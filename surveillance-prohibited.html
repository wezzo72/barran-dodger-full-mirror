The notice

Formal Notice of Non-Consent. Cease and desist: surveillance, electronic interference and digital privacy violations. Issued 18 July 2026. Address: 55B Archbold Road, Long Jetty, New South Wales.

The published text says it formally withdraws consent to all surveillance, electronic interference, recording, monitoring, harassment, and digital privacy violations at that address. It says the accommodation was without a signed contract, and that the arrangement carries no lawful authority over communications, movements, devices, or privacy. It says consent was formally and explicitly withdrawn.

The formal demand, section 5, as published:

Any person or entity engaging in the unlawful conduct described within this notice is formally demanded to immediately cease and desist; remove any unauthorised access or monitoring mechanisms; preserve all relevant records; prevent further unauthorised interference; and respect privacy, dignity, autonomy, and lawful rights.

The instruments named on the notice are the Surveillance Devices Act 2007 (NSW), the Telecommunications (Interception and Access) Act 1979 (Cth), the Criminal Code Act 1995 (Cth), the Cybercrime Act 2001 (Cth), the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), the Crimes Act 1900 (NSW), the Australian Human Rights Commission Act 1986 (Cth), and articles 7, 9, 17, 19 and 2 of the International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights.

The breach

A locked door is not a release from the notice I had already served. Surveillance after 18 July 2026, at this address, is surveillance after consent was withdrawn.
