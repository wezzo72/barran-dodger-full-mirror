Offer the Government Its Own Record

The title is Offer the Government Its Own Record. The subtitle is The man in every file — yet nowhere in the whole. It is dated 11 October 2026. It is on this site atOffer the Government Its Own Record. The same paper is on the public archive atoffer-the-government-its-own-record-11-october-2026.

The line in it is that I hold the evidence in the government’s own words, on its own letterheads, over thirty-five years, and that I do not need its permission for the record to exist. Freedom of information is the citizen asking the government what it holds. I asked for all the information government agencies hold on me. On 14 April 2022, FOI/2022/045, the answer was that the documents could not be found or do not exist. I already held the letters. The file, with the numbers, isthe FOI file. A letter proves what that letter says. The paper is the offer. It is not a finding.
