001 — Administrative annihilation

Meaning and forensic purpose

Administrative annihilation, in this report, is the name for a life being stripped of practical standing by decisions that do not have to imprison a person to remove the ordinary means of being heard, paid, housed and answered. It is an analytical description. It is not a criminal charge. It is used because “a file was closed” does not say what the closing did.

The witness’s testimony

The Federal Court letter records what Dr McLean put in the email of 7 March 2023 to Sia Lagos: that he wanted his struggles, oppression, victimisation and detriments, in psychological, financial, reputation and other ways, to be acknowledged and compensated. That is the Court writing down his demand. It is not the Court finding that the oppression occurred.

Documentary chronology

15 November 2021, the Commonwealth Ombudsman closes complaint 2021-103546 Commonwealth Ombudsman complaint 2021-103546. 3 November 2021, the OAIC files a courtesy copy and does not act. 5 November 2021, APRA refuses to treat the matter as a public interest disclosure and says it will no longer respond. 27 March 2023, the Federal Court decides not to allocate the disclosure. 16 October 2024, NCAT commits management of the estate to NSW Trustee and Guardian. The dates are on the documents. The pattern is not a conspiracy finding. It is a sequence of doors shutting.

Named documentary evidence and independent verification

PROPOSITION: official bodies received the complaints and then ended their own processes.

SOURCE AND PUBLIC VERIFICATION: E-002, E-004, E-003, E-001, E-005.

RELEVANT PASSAGE: the Ombudsman writes “your complaint file remains closed”. APRA writes that it “will no longer be responding” unless something falls inside its remit. The Court writes that “no further action under the PID Act will be taken” on the material given to the Court, and that the principal officers will not be advised. The Trustee certificate certifies that management of the estate was committed on 16 October 2024 and “remains under management”.

EVIDENTIARY SIGNIFICANCE: each record proves its own decision. Together they prove that, across four years, oversight, a regulator, a court process and a tribunal outcome all ended in the person no longer holding the process.

LIMITATION: none of these pages says the word annihilation. None of them finds that the agencies coordinated. A closed file is not proof that the underlying complaint was false, and it is not proof that every allegation inside it was true.

Forensic examination

The institutions’ own words are the record of what they did. The Ombudsman declined the Comcare issue because an Administrative Appeals Tribunal review existed, and declined other bodies because they were outside jurisdiction. APRA said the matters were not within its remit. The Court assumed, for the initial assessment, that the disclosed conduct was disclosable conduct, and then did not allocate the disclosure. The Trustee certificate is not a complaint outcome. It is a legal taking of financial management. Treating that stack as ordinary “case closure” hides the human fact that the person is left without the process and, from October 2024, without management of his own estate.

Human consequences

What these records establish as consequence is procedural and financial, not a medical finding. A closed complaint, a regulator that will not write again, a disclosure that will not be allocated, and an estate under management, are the removal of ordinary levers: an answer, a continuing investigation, control of money. The Ombudsman letter also records his report that Centrelink had moved him from the Disability Support Pension to Jobseeker and that he could not meet the job-search condition. That is his report, recorded by the Office. The Office did not decide whether the move was lawful. It told him to complain to Centrelink first.

Human-rights and legal significance

ICCPR article 2 is the right to an effective remedy. A letter that closes a file, or refuses jurisdiction, is not itself a breach. It becomes a human-rights concern when the person is repeatedly sent on and the harm he describes is never examined. CRPD article 12 concerns equal recognition before the law and support for legal capacity. A financial-management order engages that article. This page does not find the order unlawful. It finds that the order exists and that control of the estate was taken. ICESCR articles 9 and 11, social security and an adequate standard of living, are engaged by the pension complaint he made. They are not decided by the Ombudsman’s refusal to investigate yet.

Evidentiary limitations

The 7 March 2023 email to Sia Lagos was not opened as its own file in this stage. The Court letter is the evidence that the email existed and of what the Court says it contained. The NCAT reasons for the management order were not in the two-page certificate.

Final forensic finding

Justified as an analytical description of the cumulative outcome, not as a legal finding of a crime. The documents prove repeated termination of processes and the taking of estate management. They do not prove a single plan. The term is the honest name for that accumulation. Calling it “a number of separate administrative outcomes” would be true and would also hide the person who had to live through all of them.

002 — Psychiatric weaponisation

Meaning and forensic purpose

Psychiatric weaponisation means the use of a real or asserted mental-health status to avoid examining a factual complaint. It is an allegation until a record shows the substitution happening.

The witness’s testimony

Dr McLean’s published testimony is that diagnosis and behaviour labels have been used to discredit the disclosure. That testimony is his. It is not established by the five records opened in this stage.

Documentary chronology

On 15 November 2021 the Ombudsman lists, among bodies outside its jurisdiction, the Health Care Complaints Commissioner, the Mental Health Care Complaints Commissioner, AHPRA, and a hospital. The list proves he had raised those bodies. It does not quote a diagnosis, and it does not show a diagnosis being used as the reason a factual allegation was rejected.

Named documentary evidence

SOURCE: E-002. The Federal Court letter, E-001, records detriments “in psychological, financial, reputation and other ways” as his request for acknowledgement. That sentence records his categories. It does not record a clinician’s opinion, and it does not record an agency saying “we will not look because of a diagnosis”.

LIMITATION: an interim behaviour support plan dated 15 March 2024 is named in the master command. It was not opened in this stage. No page of it is quoted here.

Final forensic finding

Not established by the records opened in this stage. The allegation is preserved. It is not softened into “communication difficulty”, and it is not upgraded into a finding. The behaviour-support plan and the clinical files have to be read before this term can be justified as documentary fact.

003 — Evidentiary erasure

Meaning

Evidentiary erasure here means a decision that takes the material out of the process that was supposed to examine it, so that the record of harm stops being a live question inside that institution.

What the documents do

APRA, 5 November 2021: the matter will not be treated as a public interest disclosure, and APRA will no longer respond on further correspondence about these matters unless something relevant to its remit appears. The Court, 27 March 2023: no further action under the PID Act on the material provided to the Court, and the principal officers will not be told. The Ombudsman: the complaint file remains closed.

That is erasure from the process, not destruction of the file. The Court says it will keep electronic versions. The documents survive. What is withdrawn is the examination.

Final forensic finding

Justified in part. E-003, E-001 and E-002 prove that the institutions kept or closed a file and stopped the examination. They do not prove that originals were destroyed. The term is right for the stopping. It is wrong if it is used to mean the papers no longer exist. They exist, and they are linked above.

004 — Institutional abandonment

Meaning

Abandonment, in this record, is not a failure to be polite. It is an institution that has been told of detriment and then announces that it will not stay with the matter.

The record

The Court was told, in the words it attributes to the 7 March email, of oppression, victimisation and detriment, and of a request for acknowledgement and compensation. It then decided not to allocate, and not to tell the principal officers. APRA said it would no longer respond. The OAIC said a courtesy copy does not start its work. The Ombudsman closed the file and sent the Comcare issue to the tribunal path and the pension issue back to Centrelink.

Each explanation can be a lawful limit of jurisdiction. The accumulation is still abandonment of the person, because nobody in these letters takes up the detriment he named. A lawful limit is not the same thing as protection.

Final forensic finding

Justified as a description of the outcome of these four processes. Sources: E-001, E-003, E-004, E-002. Not justified as a finding that any officer committed an offence by applying a jurisdictional limit. The human fact remains: after the letters, he was not under those processes anymore.

005 — Procedural foreclosure

Meaning

Procedural foreclosure is a door closed by a rule before the factual allegation is decided.

The record

The Ombudsman refused to investigate the Comcare refusal because “there is a structured review process available” at the AAT, and because the Office is “not a merits review body”. It refused the pension issue until a formal complaint had been made to Centrelink. It refused a long list of Victorian and professional bodies as outside jurisdiction. APRA refused because the matters were “not within the remit of APRA”. The Court refused allocation because it was not satisfied the disclosure was an internal disclosure to an authorised recipient. The Court also wrote that it was difficult to say whether the belief was on reasonable grounds, and then assumed disclosable conduct for the initial assessment anyway. The foreclosure is procedural. It is not a merits finding that the conduct did not happen.

The unresolved tension inside the Court letter

On page 2 the General Counsel writes that he is satisfied Dr McLean is or was an employee of the Department of Social Services, trading as Rich McLean, arts and life coach, peer-support worker and mental-health advocate. On the decision page he writes that he is not satisfied the disclosure was made by a public official or provided to an authorised recipient. The letter explains the authorised-recipient point: the conduct, he says, does not relate to the Federal Court, the FCFCOA or the NNTT. It does not explain how the earlier satisfaction that there was a DSS employment sits with the later phrase “not satisfied that the disclosure was made by a public official”. That tension is in the letter. It is not an invention of this report. A reader can open {a(FC,'the PDF')} and read both sentences.

Final forensic finding

Justified. These records are foreclosure of a process, not determinations of the facts he raised. A referral to the AAT, a direction to complain to Centrelink, and a decision not to allocate are not protection and they are not findings that the complaint was empty.

006 — Administrative attrition

Meaning

Attrition is the wearing down produced by having to begin again at each new office, each of which ends its own slice and leaves the rest untouched.

Chronology actually read

2021: OAIC will not act on a courtesy copy. APRA will not treat the report as a disclosure and will stop replying. The Ombudsman closes 2021-103546 and divides the complaint into pieces it will not investigate. 2023: the Court identifies three heads of disclosable conduct he believes he has shown — perverting the course of justice, maladministration, and danger to health or safety — assumes those are disclosable for the initial assessment, and then allocates nothing. 2024: the estate is placed under management. The person is still the same person at the end of the sequence. The offices are not.

Final forensic finding

Justified as a description of the sequence. It is not justified as proof that the offices agreed to wear him down. Separate jurisdictional limits can produce the same exhaustion without a common plan. The exhaustion is real either way. The documents are the proof of the sequence, not of a pact.

007 — Political and geographic exile

Meaning

Exile in his testimony means being unable to remain safely established in the place and life he had, including the move between Victoria and New South Wales and the conditions he describes at Long Jetty. Geographic exile would require a restriction on presence or a forced displacement. Political exile would require that the displacement was produced by the exercise of public power against him.

What this stage can say

None of the five documents opened today is an order banishing him from a state. The Trustee certificate places the estate of Barran Resonance Dodger under NSW Trustee and Guardian. That is a New South Wales legal status over property and finances. It is not an exile order. His statement that he lives at Long Jetty is his location as published on this site. It is not, by itself, exile.

Final forensic finding

Not established, on these five records, as a legal exile. The testimony of displacement stands as testimony. It is not deleted. It is not converted into a finding that a minister ordered him out of Victoria, because that order was not in the documents read today. The financial-management certificate is a different and proven constriction: control of the estate, not banishment.

008 — Economic and professional erasure

Meaning

The removal of income, work, and the power to decide one’s own money.

What is documented

The Ombudsman letter records two economic complaints in his own report to that Office: Comcare’s refusal of a workers’ compensation claim, and a move from the Disability Support Pension to Jobseeker which he said left him unable to meet mutual obligations. The Office did not investigate either on the merits. The APRA letter records that he had pointed APRA to his site and to other correspondence, and that APRA would not treat him as a whistleblower under the PID Act or the Corporations Act. The Court letter records that the General Counsel was satisfied he is or was a DSS employee under the trading name of his practice as an arts and life coach, peer-support worker and mental-health advocate. That sentence is evidence that a Commonwealth official accepted, on the information given, that there had been that work. It is not a full employment history. The Trustee certificate is the hard economic record of this stage: from 16 October 2024 the estate is under management, and it was still under management when the certificate was signed on 20 January 2026.

What is not documented here

No payslip, no Comcare determination, and no Centrelink decision was opened in this stage. The amounts are not invented.

Final forensic finding

Justified in part. Loss of control of the estate is documentary fact: E-005. Loss of the compensation claim and of the pension rate is his report, recorded and not investigated: E-002. The fact of former DSS-connected work is the Court’s satisfaction on the material he supplied: E-001. A total dollar loss is not established.

009 — Architecture of silence

Meaning

Silence, in these papers, is not the absence of a letter. The letters exist. Silence is the decision, written down, to stop answering, to not tell the principal officer, and to file a copy without starting the work.

The sentences

OAIC, 3 November 2021: the Office does not initiate action on enquiry letters that are courtesy copied to it. APRA, 5 November 2021: APRA will no longer respond on any further correspondence regarding these matters unless further information relevant to its remit becomes available. Federal Court, 27 March 2023: the principal officers will not be advised of the disclosure or the decision. Ombudsman, 15 November 2021: the PID team has responded on the ASIO concern and “our team will not contact you further about these issues.”

Four written decisions to stop. That is an architecture. It is built out of jurisdictional sentences. It does not require a secret instruction to be visible.

Final forensic finding

Justified as a description of the written decisions to cease. Not justified as proof of a command from a single office. The silence is on the page of each letter. A reader does not have to take this essay’s word for it.

010 — The Witness Remains

Meaning

The sentence is the preservation principle of this archive. The person is still here. The documents are still openable. A closed complaint did not close the record.

Why it belongs in a forensic report

E-001 to E-005 were downloaded from the public repository on 11 November 2026, hashed, and read. The Court said it would retain electronic versions. The archive has published those versions. The witness’s name is on the Trustee certificate as Barran Resonance Dodger, and on the Court and Ombudsman letters as Dr Rich McLean and Dr McLean. The same person is identifiable across the records. That continuity is the opposite of erasure.

What the phrase is not

It is not a finding that every allegation in the archive is true. It is not a spiritual proof. The spiritual sense he gives the sentence, that God preserved what institutions tried to erase, is his faith. Faith is not a finding. The documentary sense is enough on its own: the files survived the closures.

Final forensic finding

Justified as the name of the record’s survival. The five documents opened in this stage are still at the links above. The witness remains because the papers remain and because he is still publishing them.
