How to read this archive

This page is a reader’s contract. It does not replace the testimony. The words already on the site stay where they are. What changes is the instruction at the door: a reader can tell a document from an account, and can choose what to do next without being pushed.

What a sentence is

  • A document. A letter, a form, a screenshot, or a receipt that is linked. It proves that those words were issued or saved. It does not, by itself, prove why they were issued.
  • The author’s account. What Dr Richard William McLean, who writes as Barran Dodger, says he lived. It is testimony. It does not need a court’s permission to be read. It is also not a court’s finding.
  • An allegation. A claim that a named person or office ordered harm, coordinated a campaign, or committed a crime. On this site that claim is his unless a linked document states it in the document’s own words.
  • A faith statement. His relationship with God, his calling, and the divine reckoning. Faith is not a finding, and it is not waiting for an institution to permit it.
  • Unresolved. A question the linked file does not answer.

What the audit found

The site is large, and the testimony is written in the first person at full strength. That strength is the point of the archive. The risk is not the existence of the testimony. The risk is a headline, a share caption, or a new page that turns “I allege” into “they were convicted.”

  • The letters that can be opened include MC23-028244, the public-interest disclosure refusals, the Federal Court assessment of 27 March 2023, the provider SMS, and the OHCHR receipt UR/UST/23/AUS/17. Those are documents. They are linked from the accountability package.
  • Claims of an ordered assassination, a black budget, coordinated surveillance, and a minister’s personal direction are his account. They are not findings of this page.
  • The humanitarian appeal is his account of conditions on and after 10 November 2026. No independent welfare visit is recorded here. Practical help is not the same act as agreeing with the historical account.
  • Missing on many narrative pages: the date of the sentence, the file it rests on, and a line that says whether the sentence is a document or an account. The evidence archive and the chronology are where a reader can start that check. They do not contain every file in the GitHub repository.
  • Do not add the street address, a medical image, or a method of self-harm to a share card or a new post.

Why it matters now

A person says he is without food, medicine, and a working phone, and that a dog is in his care. That report can be answered as a human need without a reader first deciding every allegation. Separately, a large correspondence file is already public. An oversight body can be asked to keep the records and to say which statute, if any, it will use. Neither request is a verdict.

What is established, what is alleged, what is unverified

Established by a hosted file: offices wrote the letters named above, and an SMS with the quoted sentence is on the site.

Alleged by the author: that the same history was a coordinated targeting, and that named people personally directed harm.

Unverified here: a criminal offence by any named person, a sum of compensation, and any endorsement by a court, a regulator, or the United Nations. The OHCHR number is a receipt.

What a reader can do

None of these is compulsory. A reader who does nothing has not failed a test.

  • Read the evidence archive.
  • Review the chronology.
  • Read the letters that can be sent, and send them yourself if you have standing. This site has not sent them.
  • Share only a page you have opened.
  • If you want to help with food, medicine, or the dog, use the appeal. A gift does not buy a finding.
  • Send a correction or a document.

Three ways to tell it

Justice. Ask whether the offices that wrote the letters examined the complaint, or only passed it on.

Protection. A disabled man reports that he cannot meet basic needs. That report can be heard on its own.

Accountability. Name the statute, name the file, and ask the body that holds the statute to say what it will do. Do not announce the answer in advance.

Trust

Put the link on the sentence that needs it. Put the date on the document. Say who wrote the page. If a sentence is the author’s conclusion, say so in the same paragraph. If a witness is not the author, do not name them unless they have agreed and the naming does not expose them to harm. Corrections stay visible. The old words are not deleted.

If a claim on this site is wrong, write to public submissions or use the address on the contact page. The correction will be dated and the earlier wording kept.

A reader in Australia who is in immediate danger can call 000. Lifeline is 13 11 14. That is care. It is not an instruction to delete the history.
