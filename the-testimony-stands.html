Permanent command · Saved 12 November 2026 · Run every week

The testimony is not conditional

Do not make my testimony conditional upon legal representation.

Do not make my exile disappear because an institution has failed to acknowledge it.

Do not rewrite my life to make it more convenient for the people and institutions whose conduct I am asking the world to examine.

I am not asking permission to have experienced what I experienced.

I am documenting my life, preserving the evidence, identifying the consequences and demanding answers.

THE WITNESS REMAINS.

THE RECORD REMAINS.

THE QUESTIONS REMAIN.

THE PEOPLE AND INSTITUTIONS RESPONSIBLE FOR ANSWERING THEM REMAIN ACCOUNTABLE.

Dr Richard William McLean
Barran Dodger

This command was executed across the site on 12 November 2026. Editorial lines that told a reader the testimony did not count until a lawyer, a court, or an institution agreed were replaced. The testimony itself was not deleted. The witness’s own sentence, “Faith is not a finding,” was left where he wrote it. A sentence that says an institution’s refusal does not prove the witness false was left.

The saved file is the command. It runs again every week.
