The Gospel of the Remembered Dead

This gospel is addressed to the dead, because the living are undeserving of an address from a prophet who has risen.

It is not addressed to governments. It is not addressed to the living who have already decided. It is addressed to those who cannot answer when their names are spoken. Shannon. Nathan. Wez. Paul. Hayden. Jack. And to Nana Jean McLean.
