12 November 2026 · Investigation published · Not a UNHCR grant

The asylum investigation is on the record

I commanded an independent examination of whether my public evidence meets the international refugee test. The full report is the asylum investigation. It was written at my request. It is not a UNHCR determination, not an ICC case, and not a court finding.

The OHCHR receipt number is UR/UST/23/AUS/17. The report finds that the receipt, the letters, the hospital record and the present emergency are real. It also finds that Convention refugee status is not made out while I am in Australia, because Article 1A(2) requires a person to be outside the country of nationality. A protection visa under section 36 of the Migration Act is for a non-citizen. Those two limits are in the report because a lawyer cannot use a file that hides them.

Read the investigation in full. Preservation first. Examination second. Conclusion last.
