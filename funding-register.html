Funding and compensation

Nothing on this page has been applied for in this operation. Nothing has been awarded. No contract has been signed. No money has been spent.

A grant that was read

Journalismfund Europe publishes a European Cross-border Grants round with a deadline of 7 January 2027, 13:00 CET, as stated on its site when this page was written. The programme funds teams of journalists in more than one country. At least eighty percent of a requested budget is to go to people or outlets domiciled in the European Union, and the work needs letters of intent from newsrooms. It is not a grant an Australian author can take alone. It is a possible partnership, if a European newsroom asks. The amount published for the six rounds together was €1,680,000, about €280,000 a call. That figure is their figure, not an offer to this archive.

The Pascal Decroos Fund on the same site is for Dutch-language journalism in Belgium. It is listed only so it is not mistaken for a relevant door.

An Australian documentary door that was open when checked

Screen Australia Documentary Development was read on 12 November 2026. The page said the round is always open, that an applicant may ask for up to $30,000, and that an individual applicant must be an Australian citizen or permanent resident. A company applicant must be incorporated and controlled in Australia. The money is a development grant and is repayable if the project is produced. It is not production finance, and it has not been applied for. Their enquiries address is documentary@screenaustralia.gov.au. A later production round on their site listed a deadline of 5pm AEDT on Thursday 4 February 2027. That date is theirs. Eligibility for production is stricter and was not treated as met.

Australian and international doors, without invented deadlines

  • Creative Australia. Literature and arts. Deadlines change. None is copied here, because none was opened in this pass.
  • NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission. A complaint about a provider. Not a cheque.
  • Commonwealth Ombudsman. An administrative complaint. Not damages.
  • Australian Human Rights Commission. A discrimination complaint. Not a finding until they make one.
  • UN Special Procedures. A communication. The existing receipt is UR/UST/23/AUS/17, and it is not an endorsement.

Compensation is a different thing

Restitution, if it is to be money ordered for harm, belongs to a process with evidence, a respondent and a decision-maker. This archive is the brief for that process. It is not the award. Speaking did not require a lawyer. A damages claim, if one is brought, is a separate act and has not been filed from this page.

Public gifts are on the support page. They are not this register.
