Podcast proposal: The Witness Remains

Premise. Each episode opens one record, reads what it actually says, and then lets the author say what he believes it cost him. The listener can hear the gap between the two.

A possible first six

  1. The letter that referred him on. MC23-028244.
  2. The message about the minister and the diagnosis. The Ben screenshot.
  3. A receipt from Geneva. UR/UST/23/AUS/17, and what a receipt is not.
  4. The provider. Able Point, Rachel, and the difference between a wage and care, as he describes it.
  5. The dog and the house. The SOS, without turning suffering into a sound effect.
  6. Faith, last. The verses, after the documents, so they are not mistaken for them.

Rules

No actor speaks words that are not on a page or in a recording he already holds. No episode ends by telling the listener who is guilty. Institutions named get the chance to respond where that can be done safely. The host says when a file was not opened.

What does not exist yet

No network has commissioned this. No budget has been approved. No episode has been published as audio from this operation. A producer who wants the paper version should read the documentary treatment and the downloads.
