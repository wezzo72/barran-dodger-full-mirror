How to label what you use

  • Documented fact: the words on an identifiable original record.
  • Official record: issued by a government body, court, bank or regulator and kept in the archive.
  • Personal testimony: what he says he lived, witnessed or was told.
  • Author allegation: a claim he makes that still needs examination.
  • Inference: a conclusion, with the reasoning shown.
  • Unanswered question: a material question nobody in authority has answered in the record.

Do not use silence as proof. Do not use the absence of a judgment as proof that nothing happened. Do not use faith as proof of a document. Do not treat a psychiatric history as a reason to ignore a letter.

What a fair approach looks like

If you report a named person, put the document in the sentence, say what it does not prove, and offer that person or office a chance to respond where it is safe to do so. He has published serious allegations. Publication here is not a conviction.
