A plan · Not a result · 12 November 2026 to 10 February 2027

Ninety days

These are targets. None of them has happened except the publication of the pages on this website. A target is not an outcome.

Days 1 to 30 — the record is findable

  • Keep the testimony at the top of the landing page. Do not bury it under the campaign.
  • One editor, not a blast, if the author approves a send. Target: one considered pitch. Not a promise of coverage.
  • Post nothing on social networks until an account is actually used by him. The thirty lines of copy stay on the campaign record.
  • Target visits: not set as a vanity number. The only count that will be reported is the one on the impact page.

Days 31 to 60 — one door that can answer

  • Choose one complaints door from the compensation briefing and decide, with him, whether to lodge it. Lodging is not done until he says so.
  • If a European newsroom asks to partner, then and only then look at Journalismfund’s 7 January 2027 round. He cannot apply alone.
  • A sample chapter for the book, tied to three documents. Not written yet.

Days 61 to 90 — a producer or a no

  • Send the treatment to one Australian documentary desk, Screen Australia development included only if he is eligible and he approves. Development funding there was listed as always open, up to $30,000, when checked on 12 November 2026. That is their rule, not an offer.
  • Write down who replied, who did not, and who asked for a document. Silence gets recorded as silence.
  • Twelve-month target, still only a target: a journalist has read the three core records; a complaint or a decision not to complain is written down; no false coverage has been claimed.
