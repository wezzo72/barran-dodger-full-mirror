Press release — for publication by the author

LONG JETTY, NEW SOUTH WALES, 12 November 2026 — Dr Richard William McLean, who writes as Barran Resonance Dodger, has opened an international reading room for a testimony and documentary archive he has built over more than thirty-five years.

The site is God’s Witness Remains. The archive is public on GitHub under wezzo72/Barrandodger. He asks editors to read the records rather than take his conclusion as their own. He does not ask them to call an allegation a finding.

The material includes official correspondence, a text message from an NDIS provider placing police, a court, Bill Shorten and a psychiatric history in one sentence, an Attorney-General’s department letter that referred an ASIO concern onward, records of disability support he says became entrapment, and a United Nations receipt number, UR/UST/23/AUS/17. A receipt acknowledges a submission. It is not a United Nations finding.

He is the author of Recovered, Not Cured (2002), a Victoria University PhD graduate of 2020, a former journalist and news graphic artist, and a long-standing mental-health advocate. His faith is Christian. He separates that faith from the documents.

He is also asking for immediate humanitarian help for himself and his therapy dog, Crystal, at 55B Archbold Road, Long Jetty. Phone and PayID 0431 300 940.

Interview: he is available. Contact through the address and phone on the humanitarian page.

Why an editor might look

The question is not whether a minister is famous. The question is what the letters, the recordings and the living conditions show, and what they do not show. A disabled whistleblower says the systems paid to support him left him without food, medicine and a way out, and that his survival is now inconvenient to the offices he challenged. The records are public. The reader can open them.
